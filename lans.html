Role & Contributions: Full-stack developer. Developed multi-level approval workflow across Department Head, Executive, and Admin dashboards. Built scheduling features and implemented logic to restrict bookings on closed dates.

Challenges & Solutions: A key challenge was coordinating the multi-level approval workflow and ensuring that bookings could not be made on restricted dates. I solved this by implementing role-based logic in both frontend and backend, integrating a calendar to visualize approvals and closed dates, and enforcing booking restrictions at the database level.

Outcomes: The system streamlined vehicle booking, provided clear visibility into scheduling through the calendar, ensured proper approval and restriction enforcement, and allowed administrative flexibility through Super Admin and Admin controls. I gained experience in multi-role systems, workflow automation, calendar integration, and secure scheduling logic.
